**ACID Properties (they might ask):**
- **Atomicity:** Transaction is all-or-nothing. If any part fails, everything rolls back.
- **Consistency:** Database goes from one valid state to another. Constraints are always satisfied.
- **Isolation:** Concurrent transactions don't interfere with each other.
- **Durability:** Once committed, the data survives even if the system crashes.

## 6. Hitachi — AI/ML Engineer

**What Hitachi does:** Massive Japanese conglomerate. Their digital division works on AI/ML for industrial solutions — energy, healthcare, transportation.

**What the role involves:**
- Building ML models and data pipelines
- Python, scikit-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch
- Data preprocessing, feature engineering
- Model evaluation and deployment

**What to prepare:**
- ML fundamentals — supervised/unsupervised, overfitting, bias-variance, cross-validation
- Python data science — pandas, numpy, scikit-learn
- DSA (moderate level)
- Your projects (especially RAG and Evaluation Dashboard)
- Your paper (directly relevant — NLP + ML for ranking)

**ML Quick Reference:**
- **Supervised:** Labeled data → model learns input→output mapping (classification, regression)
- **Unsupervised:** No labels → find patterns (clustering, dimensionality reduction)
- **Overfitting:** Model memorizes training data, fails on new data. Fix: regularization, more data, simpler model
- **Bias-Variance Trade-off:** Simple model = high bias, low variance. Complex model = low bias, high variance.
- **Cross-validation:** Split data K ways, train on K-1, test on 1, rotate. More reliable than single train/test split.
- **Precision:** Of everything you predicted positive, how many were actually positive?
- **Recall:** Of everything actually positive, how many did you catch?
- **F1 Score:** Harmonic mean of precision and recall
